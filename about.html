About

Version 2.2

MTG Decks is a personal Commander deckbuilding tool. Drop a decklist on the homepage, lay cards out on a canvas, organize them into categories and stacks, set a commander, and check stats as you build.

Card data and images come from Scryfall. This site is not affiliated with Wizards of the Coast or Scryfall.
