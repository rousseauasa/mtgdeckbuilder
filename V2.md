## VERSION 2
This is a big overhaul to the tool, adding massive new features.

## v2.0
- Add a card details page:
    - When you double click on a card on the canvas, a popup window of the card opens
    - The popup has the image displayed larger on the left side so you can read the text better
    - The popup on the right side has the card details displayed
    - There is a vertical divider line in the middle
    - There is an X button in the top right corner to close the popup

## v2.1
- Trash Can
    - Is located in the bottom right corner of the canvas (will have to move based on whether or not the sidebar is open)
    - If you drag and drop anything over it (cards, categories, notes, etc.) that element will be deleted.

## v2.2
- Addition of an "About" page
    - Replace on the navbar where it says "Credits" to be "About", that will take to a new About page. On the About page, at the bottom, is the Credits section, formatted as it is right now.
